// Why now

Agents already pay for things. What they lack is a budget.

TRM Labs counted about 199 million x402 transactions — 52.7 million dollars, almost all in USDC — between May 2025 and mid-2026. Most were tests and bots, not yet agents at work: the rails to pay arrived before the agents did.

The rails to pay exist. Ash is the layer above them: who sets the budget, where it may go, and the record that proves it held.

Rules that hold.

Think of a corporate card shared by four people. No one loosens their own limit: each passes down a limit equal to or below the one they received.

Per-payment, per-window and lifetime limits apply at once, with a list of allowed destinations and tokens. Tightening any of them counts from the next payment.

The rules live in a program on the network. Not on our servers, and not in the agent’s prompt.

03

Proof anyone can check.

Each payment extends a hash chain on-chain. An auditor, a partner or an LP confirms nothing was left out without taking our word for it.
